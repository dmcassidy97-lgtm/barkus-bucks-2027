The Itinerary

A loose plan, sacredly enforced. Subject to change based on how Thursday goes.

Standing Daily Orders

These apply every single day, no exceptions, no appeals.

⏰ 1 beer before 10, or 10 beers before 1. Your call. Choose wisely.
⚖️ Court sits daily @ 10am. Fines are read out, disputed (badly), and issued. All fines must be resolved — punishment served or fine paid — by the end of that same day. No sleeping on it.

⏰ 1 beer before 10 / 10 before 1 is live from the moment you land.
⚖️ Court: no session tonight — everyone gets one night's grace. Court opens for business tomorrow at 10am sharp.
DAY 2 · FRIDAY 15 JANUARY

Pickleball, Pres, and Chasing the Chicken

⏰ 1 beer before 10 / 10 before 1. ⚖️ Court @ 10am — today's fines must be squared away by midnight.
  • 11am–1pm — Pickleball Tournament. Teams are randomly assigned on the day (no picking your ringer). Straight knockout bracket through to a Grand Final. Matches are kept short and brutal — nobody's here for a three-set marathon at 11am with a hangover.
  • 1pm–2:30pm — Pres back at the house. Recover, rehydrate (with beer), get dressed for the evening.
  • 3pm — Chase the Chicken. See rules below. This is not optional.

🐔 How to Chase the Chicken

Barkus ("the Chicken") heads off alone to a pub somewhere in the district and sits down to an unlimited tab — his drinks, on him, until he's found. Everyone else splits into the same pairs from the pickleball bracket and goes hunting. Rules of the hunt:

  • Every pub you walk into to check for Barkus, your pair must have one drink before you're allowed to leave — no drive-by checks through the window.
  • Pairs can compare notes, but no group texting Barkus' location out of pity.
  • The game ends the moment everyone has found him — at which point the whole group sits down and drinks with Barkus, on his tab, until it's closed out.
  • Last pair to arrive owes Barkus a shot as a "sorry we're slow" tax.

Paintball & Rooftop Chaos

⏰ 1 beer before 10 / 10 before 1. ⚖️ Court @ 10am — settle up before you paint anyone.

  • Regroup, shower (mandatory, ask nicely if someone skips it), togas back on.

🌆 VIP Rooftop / Sunset Booking — Skyloft

Recommended venue for the sunset-into-nightclub leg: Skyloft, an open-air rooftop that runs more like a nightclub than a bar — concert-grade sound system (upgraded late 2025), international and local DJs on rotation, and unobstructed Saigon skyline views to watch the sun go down before it turns into a full dance floor. Book a table/VIP section in advance for a group this size.

Backup option: Zion Sky Lounge & Dining — tiered rooftop seating facing an open DJ booth, daily DJs, themed nights, and bottle service, if Skyloft's out.

DAY 4 · SUNDAY 17 JANUARY

The Pub Crawl vs. Barkus (Bucks Pub Golf)

⏰ 1 beer before 10 / 10 before 1. ⚖️ Court @ 10am — last full day, don't let the fines pile up.

A pub crawl, but everyone (including Barkus) is playing golf against him at the same time. Simple format, designed to survive being explained at 2am:

⛳ Bucks Pub Golf — Scoring

  • The course: each pub on the crawl is a "hole." Before drinking, the group assigns that hole a par based on the drink: shot = par 1, can/small beer = par 3, full pint = par 5.
  • Your score: the number of sips/gulps it takes you to finish the drink. Finish in fewer than par = birdie (−1 to your total). Exactly on par = par (0). Take longer = bogey (+1 per sip over).
  • Playing Barkus: Barkus plays every hole too. Beat his score on a hole and he owes a forfeit shot from his own tab. Lose to him and you pick up an extra penalty stroke.
  • Trick-shot holes: at two random pubs, draw a challenge card before drinking — "no hands," "wrong hand only," "sing the national anthem first" — and that hole's score counts double.
  • Winning: lowest total score across the crawl skips the final round of shots. Highest total score ("the Duffer") cops the group's pick from the Punishments list as a closer.
DAY 5 · MONDAY 18 JANUARY

Pack Up & Ship Out

⏰ 1 beer before 10 / 10 before 1 — one last chance for glory. ⚖️ Court @ 10am — final session, all outstanding fines must be cleared before anyone leaves the country.
  • Pack up, check out, nurse whatever's left of yourselves.
  • Final group breakfast — a chance to relitigate the entire trip over greasy food.
  • From here: most people head home. Those continuing onward peel off for the Ha Giang Loop (see below).

🛵 Ha Giang Loop Extension (Optional)

For the legends not going home yet. Rough outline — details to be firmed up closer to the date.

  • Monday, mid-morning: depart Saigon for Hanoi, then straight onto an overnight bus up to Ha Giang.
  • Tuesday–Friday: the Ha Giang Loop itself — mountain passes, motorbikes, and views that will almost make up for how much your body hates you right now.
  • Friday night: overnight bus back down to Hanoi.
  • Saturday afternoon: fly home from Hanoi.
