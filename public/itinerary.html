🐔 Chase the Chicken

Barkus sits alone at a pub on an unlimited tab. Everyone else splits into pickleball pairs and hunts him down.

  • Every pub you check, your pair has one drink before leaving.
  • No group texting his location.
  • Game ends when everyone's found him — then it's drinks on his tab.
  • Last pair owes him a shot.

DAY 4 · SUNDAY 17 JANUARY

The Pub Crawl vs. Barkus (Bucks Pub Golf)

A pub crawl, but everyone plays golf against Barkus at the same time:

⛳ Scoring

  • Par: shot = 1, can/small beer = 3, pint = 5.
  • Score: sips to finish. Under par = birdie (−1). Over = bogey (+1/sip).
  • Vs. Barkus: beat him, he owes a shot. Lose, you take a penalty stroke.
  • Trick holes: 2 random pubs — "no hands," draw a challenge, score doubles.
  • Winner skips the final round. Duffer cops a pick from the punishments list.
