Fines & Punishments

Justice is swift, biased, and settled by 10am court the next day.

How this works

See someone break a rule? Log it below — anyone can submit a fine, no permission needed. It goes straight to the ledger for Court to review at 10am. The full fines log is admin-only (organizer eyes only), so submit in good faith and let the judge sort out the drama.

Standard Punishments

Whoever's holding court picks the punishment to fit the crime — each one's got a price tag too. Max $10 total per person, per day — once someone's maxed out for the day, further fines still count on the record (and the humiliation still applies), they just stop costing more money.

  • $1The Sash of Shame Wear the sash until someone else gets fined and takes it off you.
  • $3Mystery Shot Bartender's choice. No questions, no chaser, no complaints.
  • $5Buy-a-Round You're shouting the next round for the whole group. All of them.
  • $1Opposite Hand Only Your next drink is non-dominant hand only, spills and all.
  • $2The Waiter's Apprentice You're on drink-running duty for the group for the next hour.
  • $1Toast in Character Propose the next toast in an accent voted on by the group.
  • $2Truth Corner Answer three embarrassing questions from the group. No dodging.
  • $3The Chicken Dance Perform it, in full, in the middle of the venue. Filmed, obviously.
  • $2Silent Treatment No talking for 30 minutes. Charades only if you need something.
  • $1The Naming Game Must refer to Barkus by his full ceremonial title for the rest of the day.
  • $5Call Mum/Dad Ring a parent and tell them you're gay. Straight face, no laughing.

Report a Fine

Open to everyone. Be specific — vague accusations get thrown out by the judge. Remember: $10 cap per person per day, so fines get automatically trimmed once someone's maxed out.
