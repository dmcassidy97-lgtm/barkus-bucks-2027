Standard Punishments

Court picks the punishment. Max $10 per person, per day.

  • The Sash of Shame Worn until the next fine.
  • Mystery Shot Bartender's choice.
  • Buy-a-Round For the whole group.
  • Opposite Hand Only Next drink, non-dominant hand.
  • The Waiter's Apprentice Drink-running duty, 1 hour.
  • Toast in Character Group-picked accent.
  • Truth Corner Three questions, no dodging.
  • The Chicken Dance In full, filmed.
  • Silent Treatment 30 minutes, no talking.
  • The Naming Game Barkus's full title, all day.
